# Fund the conditions. Maybe become lore.

ZeroRoomLab is seeking **Non-Exclusive Seed Stewards**: people or organizations willing to help carry the early economic responsibility of an open research lab, mythology/worldbuilding commons, rural fabrication space, and experimental AI architecture without purchasing exclusive control over them.

We do not promise profit, repayment, equity, delivery, technical success, exclusivity, official authority, or media exposure.

We do preserve transparent use-of-resources records and public provenance where privacy, safety, third-party rights, and security allow it.

A real contribution may become part of the story's provenance on GitHub, the Web, X, YouTube, TikTok, livestreams, films, music videos, or project mythology. That possibility is not guaranteed and is not a purchased advertising placement.

Funding does not buy a vote or change upstream direction. No patron must own all risk: responsibility may be bounded and shared, and patrons remain free to diversify their support portfolios. Our mythic honor ledger records provenance—no more and no less.

> No throne is promised. You may still become part of the provenance.

The vehicle-to-home analogy is Prius/PHV-style household power extraction; it does not identify a Prius as installed equipment.

The previously reported **~980 Wh is nominal battery storage**, not generation output or a single charge that lasted a month. The system secures reduced power for **survival, communications, lobbying, and NSFW video distribution**. **Research equipment is fully shut down.** Continuing tablet/phone-based documentation or GitHub work is not a restart of the local lab.

**Survival continuity is not productive independence.** Contributions of generation and charging capacity, distribution equipment, batteries, power conversion, diagnostics, field expertise, and restoration funds can reopen research branches. Portable energy logistics and the founder's labor remain dependencies; the workaround does not remove the funding or infrastructure bottleneck. Other ongoing infrastructure and legal costs are not erased. No specific delivery or return is promised. ## Infra-FIRE and the $1,000/month OSH publishing target

Infrastructure FIRE (Infra-FIRE) is our experimental route to reduce mandatory cash dependence for essential services through owned infrastructure, lawful resource access, and reproducible maintenance. It does not mean financial independence has already been achieved. The lab's land, well, water-system fabrication and sneaker-grid 0.1.x survival operations, with research equipment fully shut down are test cases, with failures, quality, legal rights and lifecycle obligations kept in scope.

**A recurring $1,000/month GitHub Sponsors target** would make it possible to budget for Git LFS or other large-file hosting, CAD/STL/3MF archiving, material and operation testing, documentation, and staged OSH publication of eligible 3D-printed water-system/well components. Git LFS has its own usage rules and existing free allowance; reaching this sponsor amount does not automatically unlock a GitHub feature or guarantee a specific release.

Sponsors fund open research capacity and independently reusable evidence, not exclusive ownership, engineering certification, delivery deadlines or veto rights. Designs with unverified potable-water, pressure or material suitability will be clearly identified as experimental.

## Legal, credit and payment-rail bottleneck (funding requirement)

The financial/legal constraints affecting the lab are also an **infrastructure dependency**. Usage-billed Git LFS or hosted storage can require a supported payment method and continued account access. Buying capacity and having the lawful, creditworthy means to pay for it are distinct gates.

The founder reports unresolved legacy legal and credit issues, with a **rough order-of-magnitude exposure of US$200,000** mentioned for legal/financial restructuring. This is a founder-reported planning figure, not an audited balance, verified settlement sum, or claim that paying that amount would automatically restore credit-card eligibility. Legal counsel would need to determine the obligations, creditor and guarantor positions, settlement alternatives, time limits, legal entity boundaries, the actual budget, and potential payment routes. We do not request a funder to assume unknown liabilities or treat a sponsorship as a debt investment.

Consequently, non-exclusive legal counsel, accounting support, lawful debt/credit restructuring and reliable payment rails are legitimate **research infrastructure funding needs**, separate from the $1,000/month recurring OSH publishing milestone. Where a card-based billing route is not available, fixed-price/prepaid storage, third-party institutional hosting under appropriate agreements, self-hosting or donated capacity are additional branches to evaluate. No bypass of identity, platform billing, or financial compliance requirements is proposed.

### Standing legal capacity must be funded before unpublished-record review

Historical Organization records and unpublished legal, intellectual-property, contract, organizational, and third-party materials are **not** a free public due-diligence bundle. A confidential source being available to the owner or a connected tool does not authorize its publication, extraction into public GitHub Issues, or distribution through external AI/RAG systems.

**The required sequence is:** fund continuing legal staffing and specialist counsel costs first; establish actual attorney engagement, confidentiality and secure review procedures; conduct a private legal/rights/disclosure assessment; publish only specifically cleared artifacts. This requires more than a one-off legal consultation. IP counsel, corporate/foundation and financial-contract counsel, evidence-handling and incident-response support may require different specialties. These resources **are not currently confirmed as funded or retained**.

Until those gates are met, nonpublic historical/legal material is `RESOURCE-GATED / NO-PUBLICATION`; public Git history and already-published claims remain available for ordinary research. A concrete legal notice, preservation requirement, or deadline should route to confidential urgent counsel, not to pre-emptive public disclosure.

Funding provides legal capacity, **not automatic access to private records, edit authority, or the right to publish**. See the [Japanese canonical release-gate policy](docs/operations/unpublished-legal-materials-funding-gate.ja.md) and [lab funding responsibilities](docs/funding/README.ja.md).

## Freedom to build beside us

Apache-2.0 code may be forked and used in proprietary products under its terms. CC BY mythology and worldbuilding material may be reused under its terms. Closed SDKs, platforms, models, and proprietary games are allowed.

Claiming official lineage, orthodoxy, canonical compatibility, or lineage-compatible MAGI status is different. A bundled system making that claim must open the complete reproducible basis of its claimed authority under the project's ShareAlike lineage policy.

This is a voluntary designation condition, not a retroactive change to Apache-2.0 or CC BY. Unless separately agreed, the upstream does not assume responsibility for independent downstream products.
