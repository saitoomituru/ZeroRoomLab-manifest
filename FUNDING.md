# Fund the conditions. Maybe become lore.

ZeroRoomLab is seeking **Non-Exclusive Seed Stewards**: people or organizations willing to help carry the early economic responsibility of an open research lab, mythology/worldbuilding commons, rural fabrication space, and experimental AI architecture without purchasing exclusive control over them.

We do not promise profit, repayment, equity, delivery, technical success, exclusivity, official authority, or media exposure.

We do preserve transparent use-of-resources records and public provenance where privacy, safety, third-party rights, and security allow it.

A real contribution may become part of the story's provenance on GitHub, the Web, X, YouTube, TikTok, livestreams, films, music videos, or project mythology. That possibility is not guaranteed and is not a purchased advertising placement.

Funding does not buy a vote or change upstream direction. No patron must own all risk: responsibility may be bounded and shared, and patrons remain free to diversify their support portfolios. Our mythic honor ledger records provenance—no more and no less.

> No throne is promised. You may still become part of the provenance.

## Field evidence: 30 days without commercial-grid power

**2026-10-15:** The operator reports that a ~980 Wh nominal off-grid reserve sustained daily living, water use, bathing, communications and lightweight creative outreach for a month after an involuntary loss of affordable commercial electricity. It did **not** sustain normal high-load technical R&D. This is a first-person field observation, not a fully instrumented energy benchmark or independent safety certification.

**Survival Independence is not Productive Independence.** Contributions of generation and charging capacity, safe power distribution, batteries, power-conversion equipment, diagnostics, field expertise, and cash for restoring dependable electricity can reopen research branches. Other ongoing infrastructure and legal costs are not erased. No specific delivery or return is promised. [Full Japanese field report](docs/projects/2026-10-15-offgrid-30days-field-report.ja.md).

## Institutional advocacy / lobbying resource branch (2026-10-18)

ZeroRoomLab also accepts **purpose-bounded support for public-interest institutional advocacy**: the work needed to bring documented OSS, gift commons, attribution and research sovereignty, rural infrastructure, and exploration dignity into discussion with local governments, elected representatives, public agencies, and other decision-makers.

Potential uses include travel and communications, preparing accessible policy briefs and evidence bundles, translation, public meetings or hearings, consultation and administrative procedures, and documenting proposals, official responses, resource flows, and outcomes. This is a **separate resource branch** from living costs, energy infrastructure, and technical R&D; no new funding, lobby access, policy adoption, or politician endorsement is asserted here.

**Supporting this branch does not buy a vote, political endorsement, preferential access, legislative outcome, or the right to determine the founder's stance.** The founder may conditionally oppose AI/science deployment under survival-threatening institutional conditions, while keeping technical facts, OSS licenses, author attribution, and each collaborator's independent political choices intact. No donor is required to adopt the founder's politics. Activities and spending must follow applicable rules for the actual form of support and activity. Where possible, keep expense categories, meetings, submitted proposals, responses, and unresolved outcomes as receipts without exposing private correspondence.

See the [Japanese conditional survival/Exit statement](docs/philosophy/exploration-dignity-survival-exit-2026-10-18.ja.md) for the author's stated constraints.

## Freedom to build beside us

Apache-2.0 code may be forked and used in proprietary products under its terms. CC BY mythology and worldbuilding material may be reused under its terms. Closed SDKs, platforms, models, and proprietary games are allowed.

Claiming official lineage, orthodoxy, canonical compatibility, or lineage-compatible MAGI status is different. A bundled system making that claim must open the complete reproducible basis of its claimed authority under the project's ShareAlike lineage policy.

This is a voluntary designation condition, not a retroactive change to Apache-2.0 or CC BY. Unless separately agreed, the upstream does not assume responsibility for independent downstream products.
