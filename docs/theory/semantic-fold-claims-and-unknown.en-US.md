# Fold / SphereOS: Misreadings and Open Questions

> [Japanese source of authority](semantic-fold-claims-and-unknown.ja.md).
> Companion to [From Meaning Topology to SphereOS and Fold-NIC](semantic-fold-os-architecture.en-US.md). This is a reader's guide. It does not silently change a kernel, JSON schema, or API.

## 1. "If FAM has ψ / ∇φ / λ / Q, isn't the World fixed at four dimensions?"

**No.** Those four keys are an open-world structural handshake. Their contents can be recursive and extensible. A `D Fold` counts semantic axes bundled under a particular World and Registry revision. That is neither the number of FAM keys, a vector embedding length, nor a claim about physical dimensions.

New axis IDs, relationships, and scopes may become known later. Declare a new mapping, Fold, or revision rather than silently editing history.

## 2. "You reject normalization, but FQuery implements normalizeFamTopology(). Contradiction?"

**Different kinds of normalization.**

- **Rejected:** irreversibly flattening unknown ontology, differing meanings, observers, and local objectives into one mandatory global-truth schema.
- **Allowed:** a **lossless local structural transformation** that makes arrays, objects, selectors, references, and revisions traversable without throwing away identity or unknown fields.

The FQuery spec's `lossless normalization` means the second one. If a shared structure can't survive local normalization without identity loss, it becomes a candidate for extraction into an independent FAM. **We're not against structure. We're against deleting meaning to make the structure look tidy.**

## 3. "Does ∇Φ mean different physical quantities for PI and SI?"

**No.** `∇Φ` is substrate-neutral architectural language for a processing gradient. Protein Intelligence and Silicon Intelligence are optional categories for participating processors or observers, not mandatory separate universes.

`ΔΦ` is a difference when the comparison has actually been defined; it is not the same as a gradient. Existing FAM specs use `∇φ`; the capital-letter notation here does not overwrite that wire token or establish a formal mathematical identity without a declared mapping.

## 4. "Are UNKNOWN, NULL, ⊥, and errors interchangeable?"

**No.** `unknown` can preserve something unclassified, unresolved, or not yet measured. `⊥`, Last Order, `not-evaluated`, `not-satisfied`, and `unresolved-selector` have different contract and stopping semantics. Don't label a missing evaluation false. Don't pretend a known unsupported edge is merely an unanswered question.

## 5. "Does a topological match prove the Worlds are the same?"

**No.** Structural fit, semantic proximity, and observed facts have different claim scopes. Do not conflate the FIT/MATCH/SELECT operations discussed in IBD, either.

```text
mapping found  ≠ scientific proof
observation    ≠ causal attribution
consensus      ≠ universal authority
reference      ≠ adoption
Fold           ≠ Merge
```

A useful cross-World mapping does not prove that two Worlds share a physical cause.

## 6. "Is Infoton a quantum particle or a physical unit of the soul?"

**No.** Infoton is a **virtualized unit of meaning** designed to preserve re-referenceable semantic, subjective, and spiritual UX content without forcing Worlds into the same ontology.

This does not dismiss spiritual meaning within the faith or experience domain. It also does not claim a newly discovered particle. Holographic cosmology, Poincaré-related mathematics, and Hamilton-related ideas are sources of mathematical or architectural inspiration. Physical evidence and proofs of formal invariants need separate work.

## 7. "Are SIOAE and PIOAE separate OAE kernels?"

**No.** The current cross-Sphere document spells OAE **Observer Agential Effect**. The Observer, Recorder, and Effect Producer are separate roles. Observations are tracked with rules, scope, and revisions.

SIOAE and PIOAE are candidate names for observer-implementation categories, not proof of completed dedicated ABIs.

## 8. "Does SphereOS centrally decide what everyone's meaning must be?"

**No.** SphereOS manages meaning identity, continuity, references, observer effects, and the interfaces for adopting, interpreting, presenting, or rejecting meanings.

**Management ≠ ownership ≠ legitimacy judgment.** One World does not gain permission to rewrite another World's canonical state just because the two can talk.

SphereOS sits above conventional execution systems. It is not claiming to replace POSIX, Linux, or hardware vendors.

## 9. "Once two Folds connect, can they share and execute everything?"

**No.** Connection, reference, transformation, adoption, and execution are different operations. A Fold-NIC transport success does not grant authority in the remote World. Cross-Fold effects still need scope, Observer, revision, receipts, and explicit handling of `unmapped_crossing`.

That is still true when an entire Sphere becomes a Fold node.

## 10. "Does any NPU already work? Will nonprofit ownership prevent all data loss?"

**Neither.** Hardware neutrality means a vendor is not the authority root of the abstract model. Drivers, adapters, and conformance tests still have to exist.

Nonprofit governance and open source address incentives and rights, not backups. Client-rich design, exports, keys, recovery, forks, and independent references have to work before provider-exit survival can be verified.

## Design questions we are deliberately leaving open

The following are **not settled by this explanation**:

1. Which properties must a mapping preserve in an M-ary dynamic graph? Where are the invariants and counterexamples?
2. What is the operational domain of `ΔΦ`, and how do we represent states that cannot be compared across Worlds?
3. What Registry/revision supplies a mapping between the historical `∇φ` and cross-domain `∇Φ`?
4. Where can PIOAE/SIOAE categories live as optional profiles without becoming forced Core enums?
5. How do we test whole-Sphere Fold identities, recursion stops, permission boundaries, and migration receipts?
6. What are Fold-NIC versus Fold8G responsibilities for namespace, IAM, transport, meaning, and shipped features?
7. What tests negotiate capabilities, unsupported operations, precision, performance, and failures for each GPU/NPU/device?
8. Which export, private-state, identity-continuity, and provider-disappearance tests prove meaning portability?
9. How do nonprofit operations, licenses, closed downstream Worlds, and upstream fork rights stay compatible?

## A reader's claim-check routine

For any claim, artifact, or implementation, ask:

- **Layer:** faith experience, narrative, design hypothesis, mathematics, measured fact, or implementation receipt?
- **Observer:** who saw what, under which rules, and are they being confused with the cause?
- **Scope:** which World, Fold, revision, jurisdiction, and runtime?
- **Status:** proposed, being corrected, implemented, tested, deployed, or retired?
- **Boundary:** did somebody erase UNKNOWN, a stopping condition, missing implementation, or missing permission?

When in doubt, don't invent missing implementation details or fake a historical OAE. Bring a concrete counterexample and a repository/revision to an Issue.
