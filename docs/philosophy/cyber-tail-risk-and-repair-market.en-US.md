# Pool catastrophic monetary risk; open a competitive cyber-repair market

Status: `[POLICY HYPOTHESIS / PUBLIC POSITION]`, 2026-10-22

## Position
**Socialize the tail; marketize improvements.** A large, broad, well-capitalized public or mutual pool absorbs **defined eligible** catastrophic, correlated financial cyber losses. This opens private insurers to build profitable additional products instead of competing to exclude risks: expedited data restoration, business-interruption coverage, specialized server remediation, contracted RTO/RPO, on-site SE response, and continuity services.

Public incident responders operate under limited capacity and priorities. A baseline ensures that waiting need not mean financial ruin, while businesses with urgent needs can purchase specialist response. Investigation by police is **not** identical to operating a universal restoration service. Customer-data duties and safe incident containment remain separate. This is a proposed framework, not a claim that governments already reimburse all stolen money.

## Separate the responsibilities
```text
Pool broadly: catastrophic, correlated monetary loss
Act locally: containment, patches, data and infrastructure recovery
Retain duties: customer-data protection, evidence, required notices
Sell privately: response time, specialized engineering, additional coverage
Settle later: allocation of financial claims and recourse
```
Liability shifting does not by itself reduce incidents. Reversible accounting losses should not gate urgent measures against irreversible damage. Competent administrators need not wait behind a paid intermediary for safe, applicable fixes.

## CVE as a shared key for remunerated repair
CVE provides shared identifiers, **not** an existing bounty or insurance payment system. A proposed market layer rewards validated discovery, coordinated disclosure, patch PRs, review, downstream packaging, deployment, and incident recovery.

The target incentive is straightforward: repairing the issue through a legitimate PR or paid support contract should yield reliable spendable income and career capital, competing favorably with the full uncertainty, logistics, laundering, and legal risk of malicious exploitation. Its behavioral impact remains a hypothesis requiring evidence.

## OpenFAPTA
Avoided losses, vulnerability research, maintainer effort, and restoration capability can be valuable even when their current market price does not capture that benefit. The analogy to oversupply-driven producer poverty separates an increase in useful capacity from monetized revenue. Treat this as a 'money bug' case for assessment of unpriced capacities, not a proven causal history of CVE.

The detailed [OpenFAPTA design case](https://github.com/saitoomituru/OpenFAPTA/blob/main/docs/theory/cve-repair-market-tail-risk.en-US.md) remains upstream of underwriting, payments, and policy enforcement. OpenFAPTA is not itself an insurer or payment rail.

## Lobbying
Separate public compensation design, commercial insurer/SI product trials, and researcher/OSS bounty programs. Evaluate the same controlled cyber-incident fixtures using payout rates, total harm, downtime, patch deployment, provider entry, and fiscal exposure. Actual outreach, conflicts, provenance, and evidence belong in a separate issue. No endorsement or policy adoption is claimed.
