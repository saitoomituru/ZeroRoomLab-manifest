# Infoton Engineering: Preserve Open Scope, Prevent Reframing Loops

This is an **en-US reading guide**, not an independent English canonical specification. Refer to the [Japanese source](infoton-abc-open-scope-agent-guard.ja.md) and the [Shannon–Weaver/OAE theory note](../theory/weaver-abc-evidence-scope-and-oae.ja.md).

## Research object before interpretive defaults

The Saito-school research program draws on Shannon's 1948 mathematical theory of communication and Weaver's 1949 distinction among Level A (technical), B (semantic), and C (effectiveness). Its independent research target is **B/C meaning and effects**, not an assertion that Shannon's work is false. Shannon also mathematically treated continuous sources; never rewrite his A-level contribution as purely digital packets.

**Do not assume in advance** that the observed system requires: a named sender/receiver, a one-way causal transmission chain, spoken/written natural language, explicit encoded packets, a particular physical carrier, or a committed metaphysical/physical ontology. Nor does leaving these variables open amount to proof that physical causation is absent or that an extraordinary mechanism works. A useful review assesses a *particular claim and test*, rather than repeatedly refuting a claim no one made.

Historical project terms included *number-theoretic quantum*, *spiritual quantum* / *spirit-world communication* for B, and *magical quantum* for C. These were **Saito-school terms, not terminology coined by Shannon or Weaver**. They deliberately cite questions from folklore, language, logos/kotodama, religion, alchemy, and black-box engineering. Current project vocabulary uses **Infoton** to protect independent research scope while preserving its source history. Do not rewrite these as claims of experimental quantum-physics hardware, or strip out their humanistic references by labeling them “mere metaphors.” Physical overlap is not ruled out, but neither presumed.

## Evidence model and implementation responsibilities

- **SELECT / Weaver A (school mapping):** scoped, discrete Fact from a source/record/predicate.
- **MATCH / Weaver B:** semantic/contextual Evidence, evaluated within OAE's observer/rule/scope.
- **FIT / Weaver C:** relational/topological structural Evidence, likewise scoped by OAE.
- **OAE:** managed observation/evaluation resource, not a source of universal authority.
- **FQuery `file.fit` and IBD FIT:** share the matching abstract data-model family, but are *different concrete implementations*.

These mapping labels are the school's proposed engineering models, not Weaver's own database API definitions. No automatic crossing from FIT to historical/source identity, or from discrete SELECT nonidentity to FIT structural negation. A single sensor record does not establish a universal environmental model.

## Stop the loop, keep scrutiny

Before an objection: quote the actual claim, list intentionally unbound variables, identify the specific evidence and observer scope, and test only the assertion actually made. If there is **no new evidence**, do not reinsert the same generic physical, regulatory, or philosophical disclaimer; continue to an experiment, source comparison, or implementation task instead. Evidence-backed contradictions and safety/rights constraints remain open to review.

Use [Japanese negative fixtures F01–F08](infoton-abc-open-scope-agent-guard.ja.md) for regression reviews.
