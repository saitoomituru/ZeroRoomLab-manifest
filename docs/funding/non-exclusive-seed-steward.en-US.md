## What this role is

A **Non-Exclusive Seed Steward** places resources into the conditions that allow ZeroRoomLab, Q Atlantis, its open-source work, its mythology, its art, and its rural research space to begin another chapter.

The steward does not purchase the upstream commons, the founder, the community, the lineage, the mythology, or the future.

In the language of Ame-no-Uzume, a steward may provide the bells, barrel, basin, light, stage, food, compute, legal shelter, archive, or room to fail that lets the sacred performance occur.

In the language of Susanoo, a steward may work beside the project, move soil, cut a channel, repair infrastructure, clear ground, or make an uninhabitable place livable.

Neither role grants ownership of the gods, the shrine maiden, the land, the story, or the commons.

Funding, withholding funding, and the size of a contribution do not change the upstream's creative, research, religious, or technical decisions. A steward receives no vote priced in dollars. A person who never funds the project remains free to fork under the applicable licenses without owning the upstream.

## What is not guaranteed

ZeroRoomLab does not promise a steward:

- technical success;
- a finished product;
- a deadline or fixed roadmap;
- repayment, profit, dividends, appreciation, revenue share, equity, debt, or tokens;
- exclusivity, veto power, acquisition rights, or preferred access;
- upstream governance, editorial control, a right to redirect the work, or a right to stop publication;
- official status, doctrinal authority, or control of the lineage;
- coverage on GitHub, the Web, X, YouTube, TikTok, livestreams, films, or music videos;
- favorable portrayal, sponsorship treatment, a character role, title, or purchased place in the mythology.

No right exists unless a separate written agreement explicitly creates it.

## What accountability remains

No guaranteed outcome does not mean no accountability.

Within the limits of privacy, safety, third-party rights, security, and contract confidentiality, ZeroRoomLab records:

- how money or in-kind resources were used;
- the date, use category, and activated branch;
- meaningful conflicts of interest and major changes of direction;
- whether the result succeeded, failed, stopped, remained dormant, or lost an external dependency;
- public provenance that can be preserved without harming people or systems.

## The mythic honor ledger: provenance, not ownership

When a contribution materially helps an event happen, and that fact belongs in the record, a steward may appear in:

- GitHub history, acknowledgments, and provenance;
- public research and production logs;
- websites, X posts, YouTube videos, TikTok videos, livestreams, music videos, or documentaries;
- the mythology as the person who supplied an instrument, preserved an archive, cleared ground, or made a chapter possible.

This is not a purchased media placement or guaranteed exposure.

We may call this record the **mythic honor ledger**: who placed what resource, and which branch or event it helped activate. It is exactly provenance—no more and no less. It is not advertising inventory, a vote, doctrinal authority, official status, lineage status, a promised result, or a purchasable role in the story.

> No throne is promised.
>
> You may still become part of the provenance.

Or, more simply:

> Fund the conditions. Maybe become lore.

## Bounded responsibility, not a single owner of all risk

No patron must carry the entire lab. Responsibility may be bounded by purpose, time, amount, loss limit, stage, equipment, legal work, preservation work, or the founder's basic runway. Several patrons may support different bounded parts at the same time.

Patrons also remain free to diversify their own portfolios across other labs, open-source projects, arts, and startups. Non-exclusivity protects their allocation choices as well as the upstream commons.

Small offerings, tips, in-kind supplies, and regional nonprofit support can keep a branch alive. They do not make small supporters the insurer of the entire lab, its legal exposure, every experiment, or every downstream product.

## A market that can carry risk

Exploratory R&D, open source, and new theories cannot promise the certainty of an already finished commodity. A market that excludes them until one actor can guarantee every outcome stops discovering new ranges.

A free market should make informed risk possible to take. Purpose limits, caps, staged funding, stop conditions, public receipts, payment disputes, insurance, in-kind support, and several independent patrons can make risk observable, divisible, stoppable, and recoverable.

When infrastructure built to handle risk instead removes exploratory actors merely because no single party owns all possible liability in advance, that infrastructure creates its own moral hazard. This position does not force a payment provider, insurer, or patron to accept unlimited risk. Each party still chooses its contract, scope, and jurisdiction.

## Why this is non-exclusive

A steward may not condition support on:

- exclusive control of open-source results;
- turning contribution size into upstream votes, direction, editorial control, or a publication stop;
- forced dependence on one cloud, model vendor, payment processor, or app store;
- ownership of mythology, personas, religious practice, or world flavor;
- sanitizing sacred NSFW, erotic, bodily, fertility-related, comic, or ritual material for investor comfort;
- shifting losses back onto small donors, a regional nonprofit, or the founder;
- closing an already public upstream through private contracts or enclosure;
- holding specifications, data, or migration paths hostage when a service ends.

A steward remains free to fork Apache-2.0 code, reuse CC BY material, build a proprietary game, create a closed platform, or develop an independent commercial world under the applicable licenses.

## Forks, products, and lineage claims

Closed products are allowed. Proprietary games are allowed. Black-box SDKs and platforms are allowed.

Attribution is open. Affiliation is conditional. Authority must be reproducible.

A product may truthfully say that it uses, derives from, or was inspired by Q Atlantis or ZeroRoomLab material under the applicable Apache-2.0 or CC BY terms.

A bundled system that claims official lineage, orthodoxy, canonical compatibility, or lineage-compatible MAGI status must disclose the complete reproducible basis of that authority, including the actual model, prompts, quarantine and audit logic, internal implementation, data, tools, tests, provenance, and build conditions, under the project's ShareAlike lineage policy.

That lineage rule is a voluntary designation profile, not a retroactive relicensing of Apache-2.0 or CC BY material. A fork that does not claim `official-lineage`, `orthodoxy`, `canonical-compatible`, or an equivalent affiliation keeps the freedoms of its applicable licenses and owes no lineage application.

Unless a separate contract or applicable law says otherwise, attribution or a lineage designation does not make the upstream responsible for a downstream product's design, operation, legality, quality, losses, customer obligations, or product and service liabilities.

See [Open Lineage / Fork / legitimacy policy](open-lineage-and-fork-policy.ja.md).

## Legal instruments are separate

This public policy does not, by itself, create a security, investment contract, donation agreement, grant agreement, loan, equity interest, debt, repayment duty, or tax classification.

Any regulated or financial instrument must be defined separately for the chosen jurisdiction, entity, amount, accounting treatment, and contract.
